Researched 2026-10-13. These are sourced facts only. Labels used:
- **[SR]**: self-reported by the founder or company.

## 5. Arithmetic: how many free users or visitors reach 35 / 350 / 3,500 paying users

**This is arithmetic, not a forecast.** The formula is required free users = target payers ÷ conversion rate. Each rate is used exactly as its source states it.

### A. Free users or installs needed (free→paid rates)

| Rate (source) | 35 payers | 350 payers | 3,500 payers |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.31%: 2/641 downloads, indie one-time-upgrade plugin [SR] | 11,218 | 112,175 | 1,121,750 |
| 0.8%: indie Chrome extension portfolio [SR] | 4,375 | 43,750 | 437,500 |
| 3%: Lenny "good" freemium self-serve, low end | 1,167 | 11,667 | 116,667 |
| 5%: Lenny "good" freemium self-serve, high end | 700 | 7,000 | 70,000 |
| 5.5%: Growth Unhinged freemium free→paid | 636 | 6,364 | 63,636 |
| 8%: Growth Unhinged median / Lenny free-trial "good" low end | 438 | 4,375 | 43,750 |
| 12%: Lenny free-trial "good" high end | 292 | 2,917 | 29,167 |

### B. Website visitors needed (visitor→payer, Growth Unhinged per-1,000-visitor figures)

| Funnel | Payers per 1,000 visitors | 35 | 350 | 3,500 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Freemium | 5 | 7,000 | 70,000 | 700,000 |
| Free trial, no card | 3.6 | 9,722 | 97,222 | 972,222 |
| Free trial, card required | 10.5 | 3,333 | 33,333 | 333,333 |

### C. Gross revenue at $10–28 one-time (before payment and processor fees)

| Payers | $10 | $28 |
|---|---|---|
| 35 | $350 | $980 |
| 350 | $3,500 | $9,800 |
| 3,500 | $35,000 | $98,000 |

Reference point: TypingMind, the comparable that matches the "~20,000 users" competitor figure (its page claims 20,641+), self-reported 4,000+ paying users in its first ~3 months, at prices rising from $9 to $79.
